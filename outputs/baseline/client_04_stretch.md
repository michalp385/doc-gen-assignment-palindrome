## Recommendations

We recommend the following:

In light of the recent sale of your business and the completion payment of £850,000 now held in a solicitor's client account, we recommend a structured approach to invest these funds in a way that aligns with your long-term growth objectives and risk appetite.

Firstly, we should utilise both of your annual ISA allowances for the new tax year. This means contributing £20,000 each to your respective Stocks & Shares ISAs on the Holloway platform. By doing this, you can take advantage of the tax-efficient wrapper that ISAs provide, allowing your investments to grow free from income or capital gains tax.

Next, we recommend making contributions to both of your SIPPs on the Brightwell platform. This will not only benefit from tax relief but also support your goal of building a retirement base that will replace James's income as he steps back from full-time work in about three years. We will size these contributions carefully to ensure we remain within the current pension allowance limits and avoid any penalties from over-contributing.

We also suggest adding further investments to the jointly-held General Investment Account (GIA) on Holloway. Given that this account has shown significant growth recently, this is an ideal position to leverage the investment. We recommend a balanced approach: taking some funds from the GIA to disinvest and rebalance, maintaining your desired risk profile while capturing gains.

Finally, we propose opening a new jointly-held investment account for any remaining proceeds that are not allocated to the ISAs or SIPPs. This account can serve as a flexible vehicle for your investments, allowing for diversification and easy access.

Overall, by following these recommendations, we aim to build a robust and tax-efficient investment base that caters to both your immediate and long-term financial aspirations.
